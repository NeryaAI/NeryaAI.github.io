# Nerya landing

## Register

brand

## Users

Strategy builders and researchers evaluating a local Agent workspace. They need to see how an idea becomes a strategy, how teammates challenge it, and how credentials and changes stay under their control.

## Product Purpose

Show the actual Nerya workspace and concise, looping demonstrations of strategy creation, multi-agent research, review/evolution and Vault security. Lead visitors into a usable product tour or local setup.

## Brand Personality

Curious, hands-on, precise. Keep the purple cat identity, distinct member portraits, bilingual copy and light/dark modes established by the existing site.

## Anti-references

The user's September 26 brief rejects ordinary explanatory modules and a paper-versus-live marketing narrative. Use joinoasis.com's media-led product storytelling as the new reference. Avoid repetitive icon cards, invented account performance, and repeated mock disclaimers.

## Design Principles

- Demonstrate a complete action rather than enumerate features.
- Keep the full source-backed Agent workspace usable.
- Use current product source to qualify security and autonomy claims.
- Make the next action obvious without long explanatory text.
- Preserve existing links and icon work during the Astro migration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Retain keyboard access, bilingual labels, mobile layouts, theme choice and reduced-motion behavior from the existing implementation. Every moving demonstration has pause/replay and a readable still alternative. Target WCAG AA contrast.
